What the app does today

Current status: no data collected

As of the date above, Brain Test does not collect, transmit, or share any personal information. There are no accounts, no sign-up, and no login. The app makes no network requests and contains no advertising and no analytics or tracking SDKs. It works fully offline.

Data stored on your device

The app saves the following only on your device, using local storage, so your game works the way you left it:

  • Your level progress and best times/stars
  • Your settings (theme, timer, confetti, one-stroke mode)

This information never leaves your device. It is not sent to Edgehog or to any third party. Uninstalling the app, or clearing the app's storage, permanently removes it.

As the app grows

Brain Test is actively developed, and future versions may introduce features that were not part of the app on the date above — for example:

Advertising

We may integrate an ad network (such as Google AdMob) to show ads in the app. Ad networks typically use an advertising identifier, general device information, coarse location (such as country or region derived from IP address), and ad-interaction data (impressions, clicks) to serve and measure ads. Some ads may be personalized based on this information unless you opt out (see Your choices).

Analytics and crash reporting

We may add analytics or crash-reporting tools (such as Firebase Analytics or Crashlytics) to understand how the app is used and to fix bugs. These typically collect device information, app version, usage events (e.g. which screens are opened, which puzzles are played), and diagnostic/crash logs. This data is generally tied to a device or install identifier rather than your real-world identity.

Accounts and cloud save

If we ever add optional sign-in (for example, to sync progress across devices), it would be opt-in, and this policy would be updated first to describe exactly what account data is collected and how it's used.

Whenever any of the above is added, we will update this policy before the feature ships, update the "Data safety" section of the app's Play Store listing to reflect it accurately, and note it in the app's release notes. We will not silently start collecting data that isn't disclosed here.

Third-party services

Brain Test currently uses no third-party SDKs. If advertising, analytics, or crash reporting is added in the future, the specific providers in use (e.g. Google AdMob, Firebase) will be listed here by name, along with links to their own privacy policies, since those companies process data under their own terms as well as ours.

Permissions

The app requests no sensitive device permissions (no location, contacts, camera, microphone, or storage-access permissions). If a future feature requires a new permission, Android will prompt you for it at that time, and this policy will explain why it's needed.

Your choices

Because nothing is collected today, there is currently nothing to opt out of. Once ads or analytics are introduced, you will generally be able to:

  • Reset or limit your device's advertising identifier in your phone's system settings (Settings → Privacy → Ads, or equivalent)
  • Opt out of personalized ads where the ad network supports it
  • Contact us (see below) with questions about data collected about your device

Children's privacy

The app is suitable for all ages. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children. If advertising is added in the future, we will configure it to comply with applicable children's privacy laws (such as COPPA) for audiences that include children, including serving only non-personalized ads where required.

Data retention & deletion

Local data (progress and settings) is retained on your device until you clear the app's storage or uninstall it — at which point it is permanently deleted. If we introduce server-side data collection in the future, this section will be updated with specific retention periods and deletion options.

International users

Brain Test can be played anywhere. Since no personal data currently leaves your device, there is no international data transfer to disclose today. This section will be updated if that changes.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this same URL with a new "Last updated" date above. Meaningful changes (such as adding advertising or analytics) will also be reflected in the app's Play Store "Data safety" section and release notes.
